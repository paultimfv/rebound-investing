# Deep-dive sections (rate each 1–5; ★ = the most important)

Claude drafts each section from the data. you sets the rating on the ★ sections.

**Business**
1. What it does, in one sentence. Who pays and why.
2. Revenue drivers (price × volume, broken down by segment)
3. Unit economics / take rate
4. TAM and share: is share growing?
5. ★ Moat: why will it stay a quality business, why is it hard to compete with, and is the moat deepening over the next few years?
6. ★ Pricing power: can it raise prices at or above inflation? Porter's 5 forces: customer power, supplier power, substitutes, new entrants, rivalry. How will each trend over the next 5y?
7. Customer concentration and churn
8. Competitive landscape: the top 3 rivals and what they're doing
9. Upstream and downstream check: what are suppliers and customers saying? (the ASML lesson)

**People**
10. ★ Management: did they steer the company through past cycles? Founder-led? Incentives aligned with minority shareholders?
11. Capital allocation track record: M&A, buybacks, dividends, ROIC on new capital
12. Insider buying and selling (tokens: team and VC unlocks, and whether they sell)

**Numbers**
13. QGV metrics over the last 5y (from qgv.py)
14. Margin trajectory: GP and OP vs revenue growth
15. Balance sheet: net debt / EBITDA, maturities, liquidity (tokens: treasury runway, and the treasury's exposure to its own token)
16. ★ Forensic accounting: CFO vs net income, accruals, DSO and inventory trend, capitalized costs, SBC as a % of FCF, non-GAAP gap, audit or restatement history (tokens: organic vs farmed fees, wash trading)
17. Dilution: share count trend, and SBC (tokens: emissions and unlocks)

**Market**
18. Why it's down: the market's story, with a date for each leg of the decline
19. ★ Short thesis: the 3 strongest reasons to short it. Refute each one, call it near-term only, or concede it.
20. What the price discounts (reverse DCF) vs consensus
21. Ownership: 13F changes, notable holders (tokens: fund wallets, smart money)
22. Key risks (regulatory, technology, key person), excluding macro

**Decision**
23. KPIs (2–3) and the catalysts, with dates
24. Bear / base / bull and the reward:risk
25. Rating, size, tranche plan, and the conditions that would change the decision (the KPI levels that break the thesis)
